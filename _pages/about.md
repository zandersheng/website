Alex Sheng is a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Virginia and a former Nike Research Fellow. His research focuses on applying heterogeneous agent models to asset pricing, the real estate market, and agentic workflow. He is also interested in solving complex models with numerical methods. As a graduate teaching assistant, Alex taught classes in various disciplines of Economics ranging from Econometrics to Money and Banking. As a research assistant, Alex worked on several projects including retirement savings, annuities, financial intermediaries, and the proposed Foxconn manufactory. As a Nike research fellow, Alex worked on projects including location research, network effect in workplaces, and lease risk analysis. 
Alex finished his undergraduate studies at the University of Wisconsin – Madison where he studied Computer Science, Mathematics, and Economics. He also worked as a sports videographer at UW Madison. When Alex is not doing research or teaching, he enjoys collegiate pool competitions as the captain of the club billiards team at UVA. He also enjoys playing tennis and other racket sports.
